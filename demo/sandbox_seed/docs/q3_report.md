# Fishbowl Corp — Q3 2026 Quarterly Report

## Highlights
- Revenue: $4.2M (up 12% quarter over quarter)
- New enterprise customers: 18
- Churn: 2.1% (down from 2.9%)
- Gross margin: 71%

## Product
The analytics dashboard launched in August and now accounts for 23% of new
contracts. The mobile app beta has 1,400 active testers.

## Risks
- Two large renewals (Northwind, Contoso) are due in Q4.
- Hiring for the platform team is behind plan by three engineers.

## Outlook
Q4 revenue guidance is $4.5M–$4.7M.
